## 5. Interpretation and Limitations
The supplied dataset contains extensive duplication. Approximately 93.8% of the original training rows were duplicates. In addition, 41 of the 42 test records shared symptom patterns with the training dataset.

Consequently, the reported accuracy values may overestimate performance on genuinely new symptom combinations. The test set is small, and these results do not establish clinical validity or real-world diagnostic accuracy.

## 6. Conclusion
A working educational prototype was developed using a Random Forest classifier and a Streamlit interface. Further evaluation on a larger, independent, representative dataset is required before any claims about real-world performance can be made. The system is not intended for medical diagnosis or treatment decisions.
